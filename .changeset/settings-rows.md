Settings rows: `SettingsSection` (card with header, count, status, actions, footer, footnote, danger tone, section lock, aside, columns and nested sub-groups, a no-JavaScript empty state), `SettingsRow` (label, description and a control wired through Field context; staged "Unsaved"/"was X"/impact inside a SettingsForm, saving/saved/error states, locks with a visible reason, actions that collapse into a menu, narrow summary mode), `SettingsFacts` (read-only facts as a description list or tiles, with sources, compact values, local copy and one action), `SettingsToggle` (instant with revert, Retry and Undo; staged inside a SettingsForm; a no-JavaScript Apply form; "Always on" badges), `RequestedChange` (a change waiting for approval elsewhere, with Send again and Cancel request, never shown as saved) and `LockReason` (one visible reason per lock kind, a way forward and a request flow, describing any number of controls).

Layout and accessibility fixes: a row's `reasonId` describes its control only for its own lock (a stronger section or page lock is described by its own reason); `layout="stacked"` puts the control under the label; `layout="inline"` (every SettingsToggle) wraps the control under the label when the label would get less than 60% of the row; a SettingsToggle switch ends on the same right edge as other controls and shows its state as text in forced-colors mode; a failed instant save says "Changed back" and no longer marks the control invalid; overflow menus are named for their row ("More actions for Ada Park", `labels.moreActions` is now a function) and show an action's `ariaLabel`. Buttons that can only call back (a callback-only action, `onRequest` without `requestAction`, RequestedChange without `action`) stay soft-disabled until the page is interactive. New `requestAction` on LockReason, SettingsSection, SettingsRow and SettingsToggle posts `intent=request-access` without JavaScript. SettingsFacts no longer carries its own inset: put it in the padded box of a `list={false}` section like its neighbours. A row with a `status` shows Saving and Saved on its label line, never beside the control, in a slot that keeps its room while idle (new `statusWords` reserves room for other `statusText`, such as SettingsToggle's "Undone"), so neither the control, the label's wrap nor the rows below move whatever the save state.
