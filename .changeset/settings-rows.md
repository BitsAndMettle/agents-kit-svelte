Settings rows: `SettingsSection` (card with header, count, status, actions, footer, footnote, danger tone, section lock, aside, columns and nested sub-groups, a no-JavaScript empty state), `SettingsRow` (label, description and a control wired through Field context; staged "Unsaved"/"was X"/impact inside a SettingsForm, saving/saved/error states, locks with a visible reason, actions that collapse into a menu, narrow summary mode), `SettingsFacts` (read-only facts as a description list or tiles, with sources, compact values, local copy and one action), `SettingsToggle` (instant with revert, Retry and Undo; staged inside a SettingsForm; a no-JavaScript Apply form; "Always on" badges), `RequestedChange` (a change waiting for approval elsewhere, with Send again and Cancel request, never shown as saved) and `LockReason` (one visible reason per lock kind, a way forward and a request flow, describing any number of controls).

Layout and accessibility fixes: a row's `reasonId` describes its control only for its own lock (a stronger section or page lock is described by its own reason); `layout="stacked"` puts the control under the label; `layout="inline"` (every SettingsToggle) wraps the control under the label when the label would get less than 60% of the row; a SettingsToggle switch ends on the same right edge as other controls and shows its state as text in forced-colors mode; a failed instant save says "Changed back" and no longer marks the control invalid; overflow menus are named for their row ("More actions for Ada Park", `labels.moreActions` is now a function) and show an action's `ariaLabel`. Buttons that can only call back (a callback-only action, `onRequest` without `requestAction`, RequestedChange without `action`) stay soft-disabled until the page is interactive. New `requestAction` on LockReason, SettingsSection, SettingsRow and SettingsToggle posts `intent=request-access` without JavaScript. SettingsFacts no longer carries its own inset: put it in the padded box of a `list={false}` section like its neighbours. A row with a `status` or `feedback` shows Saving, Saved, Undo and Retry on a status line that is the row's last line, after the control and as tall as a small button: it takes no room on the label line, so labels wrap only as they would anyway, and coming or going it never moves the control, beside the label or wrapped under it. A `leading` icon sits in a 2rem column, so labels and the status line line up across rows. A SettingsToggle's On/Off text is as wide as its longer word, so flipping never moves the switch. A SettingsRow passes the lock that applies to it (and its reason) to its control, so a ConsequenceDialog inside a locked row shows the row's own reason.
