Quiet chrome. `ChatWorkbench` takes `chrome="quiet"` (default `"full"`, today's markup): the header band keeps only the phone's tabs and New conversation and is hidden from 1024px in `layout="auto"`, so the rails start at the top; `title`, `subtitle`, `status` and `headerActions` become one baseline-aligned line atop the chat column, at the message text's inset, drawn only when one is given, its right side truncating (`headerMark` is ignored). The title keeps its room: the status truncates first, then the actions give way, each allowed to shrink, so an action with `min-w-0` and its text in a `truncate` span (`class="{button} min-w-0"`) cuts that text short, while one without keeps its width. Every conversation row gets one 14px leading slot (plus, pin, dot or nothing) so titles share an edge; idle titles are `text-fg-main`, the active row has no shadow, day labels and dates are 12px. In the sidebar, rows are 32px with a mouse (`pointer-fine`) and day labels 32px blocks, so every pitch is 32px; touch screens and the tab panel keep 44px rows. `empty` is drawn just above the composer (without it, "No messages yet." stays in the log), and the composer is compact. `PromptInput` takes `compact`: a one-line text field (`min-h-8`) and tighter padding, still growing to `max-h-60`. `ChatWorkbenchConversation` takes `pinned`, a pin in place of the mark, in either chrome, with the row's second line starting at its title. `CommandPalette` takes `triggerCompact`: the default opener becomes a 32px ghost button with the search icon and the shortcut as text from 640px, named by `triggerLabel`.
