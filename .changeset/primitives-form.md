Primitives: form controls. `Field` gives one control its label, hint, error and required or optional mark; the kit controls inside it take its id, description, invalid and required state by themselves, and its children snippet hands the same attributes to any other element. The error is announced politely when it appears, and `<Field group>` renders a fieldset and legend for a RadioGroup or several checkboxes. `Input` (sizes, leading and trailing content, a clear button), `Textarea` (auto-grow, a character count that speaks only near the limit), `Checkbox` (indeterminate), `Switch` (`role="switch"`), `RadioGroup` (built on the headless RadioGroup), `NativeSelect` and `Slider` are native controls that work in a plain `<form>`: they post their name and value, `required` blocks a submit, and a form reset restores the value they started with. All support `bind:value` or `bind:checked` plus `onValueChange` or `onCheckedChange`. `getFieldContext` and `fieldControlAttrs` let your own controls join a Field.
