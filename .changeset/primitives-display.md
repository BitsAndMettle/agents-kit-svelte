Adds the display primitives, exported from `agents-kit-svelte/primitives`: `Badge`, `Avatar`, `AvatarGroup`, `Card`, `Separator`, `Kbd`, `Skeleton`, `Spinner`, `Progress`, `Alert` and `EmptyState`. Avatars fall back to initials or an icon when the image fails, a clickable `Card` has a single tab stop, `Alert` is announced only when you set `live`, and `Skeleton` and `Spinner` stand still under reduced motion.
